## Overview

This dataset contains racism-related tourism and hospitality reviews collected from the Internet up to February 2019. There are 4554 reviews in total.

## Files

- `Racism2019.csv`

Column descriptions are retained in the data dictionary below.

## Source and provenance

The reviews were collected from the Internet and used in Li, Li, Law, and Paradies (2020). Specific review-source sites, collection terms, privacy constraints, and attribution requirements are not fully documented in this README.
